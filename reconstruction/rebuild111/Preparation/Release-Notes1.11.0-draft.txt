MYRO v1.11.0 — FULL CAMPAIGN UPDATE
DRAFT: not a packaged release; normal checks/reviews/packaging gates still apply.

REQUIRED PREVIOUS VERSION
Use the released 1.10.0 ISO only. Earlier versions, modified images and private
builds are not accepted. The updater checks the complete baseline and payload.

INSTALLATION
Extract the entire ZIP, then run Apply-Update.cmd and select your 1.10.0 ISO.
The updater builds MyRO-Current.iso and keeps the previous accepted image for
rollback. Use the rebuilt image with your usual Xbox or Xemu installation.
For a folder installation, transfer ALL files from the rebuilt image, including
guildviews.bin. Replacing only default.xbe is insufficient.

IMPORTANT CHANGES (SPOILER-FREE)
- The full twelve-chapter campaign is enabled with its authored encounters,
  evidence, interactions and companion recruitment/progression.
- Companions earn their own EXP/levels, follow individual preset career paths,
  and require story milestones for promotions. Health, SP and behavior persist.
  Up to three accompany you; you continue controlling your own character.
- Guild construction, improvements and original furnishings are restored.
  All six original arcade games and saved scores are included. Cabinets now
  have eight camera directions, retaining their established designs.
- Regional dispatches and campaign-aware map/NPC access are integrated.
  Native job quest costs/rewards and existing offline content are preserved.

CHARACTERS AND SAVES
Start a fresh character for this complete-campaign playthrough. Obsolete testing
characters are intentionally outside migration/preservation requirements.
The updater does not edit Xbox save files. Current character saves use the
recovered development namespace; exact paths are documented in the report.

WHAT TO TEST ON YOUR XBOX
Save and restart early, then confirm your character and companion health/SP,
levels and behavior settings. Check that changing formation does not refill
health/SP. Play through campaign entrances, objectives and recruitment in order.
Try guild construction, all six cabinets, rotating the camera around them,
regional dispatch rewards, and travel denied by funds or progression.
On a 64 MB Xbox, watch for memory pressure, slowdowns, audio interruptions or
instability during busy maps, travel and sustained three-companion combat.
Report the nearest spoiler-free journal objective and steps if anything fails.

VERIFICATION LIMITS
Detailed normal checks, the two additional reviews and patch reconstruction
results belong in the accompanying report once complete. Host checks, native
compilation and native CPU comparisons are not Xbox or Xemu playtests.
No original-Xbox or Xemu playtest is claimed for this recovered candidate.
