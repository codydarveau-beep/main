RAGNAROK OFFLINE 1.11.1 — GUI AND PRESERVATION RESTORATION

REQUIRES: the exact released 1.10.0 ISO.
This patch does not accept 1.11.0, private builds, or modified images.
Use your retained released 1.10.0 image as the input, even if you have tested 1.11.0.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder on your PC.
2. Double-click Apply-Update.cmd and select your released 1.10.0 ISO.
3. Wait for SUCCESS. Use the resulting MyRO-Current.iso.
   If an unrelated MyRO-Current.iso is already in that folder, move it first.
4. Transfer/use the rebuilt game with your usual Xbox or Xemu setup.
   Start a new character for this restoration test, as planned.

The updater checks the complete input and output. It preserves a rollback
image and creates a receipt. Do not delete payload.001.bin before updating.

IMPORTANT CHANGES
- Restored approved OFFLINE title artwork and the released HUD atlas.
- Fixed shared UI vertex ownership behind creator/name-window flicker.
- Restored icon atlas sampling to prevent neighboring-pixel edge artifacts.
- Character creation uses a spatial stat web: A enters; D-pad selects;
  X raises and Y lowers; A keeps the edit and B cancels it.
- Restored X stat allocation, equals column, hints and clearer row layout.
- Equipment entries use one line; the larger centered preview faces forward.
- Raised hotbars and restored active-bar ordering during assignment.
- Restored original water and Screen draw defaults under Diagnostics >
  Video Testing. Diagnostics remains available without Developer Mode.
- Quest A opens details; X tracks/untracks. Dialogue objectives have no
  false progress bar; real counters keep their progress bars.
- Restored readable HP/SP/EXP bars, percent-only EXP, warning digits without
  black outlines, and hidden enemy HP with an optional diagnostic toggle.
- Repaired tutorial button graphics/prose, blank pages and outdated lessons.
  Basic Skill retains its /9 maximum while earning ranks.
- Restored the training graduation NPC and reachable interaction range.
- Restored larger damage/MISS presentation and critical fitting; delayed
  Bash and ordinary attack results to their contact point, retaining multi-hit timing.
- Rebuilt Willow/Elder Willow deaths from original animation layers with
  shared bounded resources instead of a blown-out clipped square.

The complete twelve-chapter campaign, independent companions, guild upgrades,
all six original arcade engines, directional cabinets, regional dispatches
and existing native quest costs/rewards remain included.

WHAT TO TEST ON YOUR ORIGINAL 64 MB XBOX
Start with character creation and the complete Training Grounds route,
including graduation. Check window entry/navigation, all inventory screens,
equipment preview, quest details/tracking and hotbar assignment. Check CRT
readability, water, Willow deaths and combat impact/audio alignment.
Then check guardians and repeated KNT01/Lighthalzen travel and traversal.
Continue campaign and companion progression from there.
