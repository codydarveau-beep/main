CART TEST ROUTE
Use Merchant class preset; rent a cart from the existing supplies merchant.
The third visible NPC near the Prontera starting area is now the Cart Stylist,
48worldunits from the centre along the other street axis, between/beyond the two
existing service NPCs. Three gold minimap service markers are shown.
Approach the third NPC and press A. Choose any of five styles for free and B to
leave. This test service bypasses level/SP restrictions; the normal Change Cart
skill keeps its existing level requirements (41/66/81/91 for higher styles).
Check stillness, movement, stop/start, all camera angles/zooms and all styles.
Idle must not bounce. Wheels should stay at the projected ground contact, with
a24-unit gap behind the player. Cart depth is sorted with characters.
