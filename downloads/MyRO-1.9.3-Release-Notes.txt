MYRO v1.9.3 — FOUR-CHAPTER STORY PREVIEW

Extract the whole ZIP, run Apply-Update.cmd and select your released 1.9.0,
1.9.1 or 1.9.2 ISO. The updater creates MyRO-Current.iso. Use your usual
Xbox transfer or xemu process. Folder installs need ALL files from the new
image, including campaign.bin, chapter1.bin and storyportraits.bin.
Saves and settings use the normal E:\MyROOffline folder.

STORY AND COMPANIONS
Start a fresh Novice without Developer Mode to play the campaign. Finish the
Training Grounds; the journal then guides you to the driver south of Prontera.
The preview covers four chapters: Prontera, Payon/Alberta, Geffen/Izlude,
and Morroc/Pyramids. It stops explicitly after Chapter 4. Story recruitment
introduces Roan, Yuufa, Judia, Maya, Brann, Takius, Pip and Iruga. Recruitment
can be postponed where offered. Up to three companions travel with you.
Open Pause > Game > Companions for the roster, settings and controller guide.
Only active, living nearby companions earn EXP; they have independent base
and job levels, personality builds and story-gated job promotions. Reserve
members do not gain EXP. Maya's Potion Pitcher consumes your Red Potions;
her skill use starts OFF so you can choose whether to spend supplies.

Developer Mode is a testing sandbox: the three humorous confirmation prompts
convert a campaign character out of normal story progression. Keep your normal
story character separate from your morphing developer test character.

UI AND COMBAT
Compact gold Main/silver Side quest groups; concise larger objectives, live
collection/kill counters and fixed-position completion/failure markers.
Use Y in the journal for full quest text, A to track or untrack.
Larger damage and Miss art; critical burst fits its centered number.
Centered larger equipment preview, compact icon/name fields and bold headers.
Hotbar quantity backing; bold current HP/SP; status equals column.
Hold X to add stats repeatedly. Developer items now have subtype menus/icons.
Item hover descriptions no longer print raw metadata or split into extra boxes.
Bash now plays the original cast/impact cues and effects, with actual damage
and numbers delayed to the attack's impact. No additional assets required.

VALIDATION
Native nxdk Xbox build and host sanitizer checks, actual packed map placement
renders, companion combat/growth/save tests and shipped PowerShell updater
verification. Original Xbox gameplay, audio and CRT acceptance remain for
hands-on testing; no hardware performance guarantee is implied.

Version 2.0.0 remains reserved for the complete campaign. The guild-base
upgrade/arcade proposal is not included in this preview.
