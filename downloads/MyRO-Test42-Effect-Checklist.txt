uses the smaller prototype bolt path. Entries16-20 are prototype compositions
from collected textures. Entry21 and the aiming ring are procedural prototypes,
not the original client's full effects/models. Exact original effect playback
remains unfinished. Existing numerical mechanics have not been replaced by
